## What fold to 3-bet measures

When you open the pot and someone re-raises, that re-raise is a **3-bet**. **Fold to 3-bet** is the share of the times a player faced a 3-bet after opening where they folded.

```
fold to 3-bet = times folded to a 3-bet / times faced a 3-bet after raising
```

It is the defensive side of [3-bet%](/blog/understanding-vpip-pfr-3bet-hud-stats) and the counterpart of [4-bet%](/blog/4-bet-percentage-poker-stat-explained).

## The three possible answers add up to 100%

Facing a 3-bet, a player can fold, call or 4-bet. Fold to 3-bet, call 3-bet and 4-bet% (on the same opportunities) together make 100%.

A worked example: an opponent opens and faces a 3-bet 25 times. They fold 16, call 6 and 4-bet 3. Their fold to 3-bet is 16 / 25 = 64%, they call 24% of the time and 4-bet 12%.

## What the number tells you

In 6-max cash games a fold to 3-bet in the region of 55 to 65% is common among regulars. As always, that is only a guide, and it depends on position and on who is 3-betting.

- **High fold to 3-bet (for example 75% or more).** The player opens and gives up when pressured. You can 3-bet them wider, including hands with a blocker or some equity that you would not use against someone who fights back.
- **Low fold to 3-bet (for example under 45%).** The player defends a lot, by calling or 4-betting. Cut the bluffs from your 3-betting range and 3-bet for value with strong hands, because they will pay you off.
- **A big 4-bet share inside the stat.** If most of the non-folds are 4-bets, the opponent is aggressive, and a good response is calling more of your strong hands to trap them.

## Position and context change it

- **In position vs out of position.** A player is much more willing to call a 3-bet in position than out of it, so the same player can show two very different folds.
- **Who is 3-betting.** Players fold more to a tight opponent and defend more against someone they know 3-bets a lot.
- **Stack depth.** Shallow stacks make folding to a 3-bet rarer, since a 4-bet is nearly an all-in.

## Using it on yourself

Your own fold to 3-bet is a leak detector. If it is very high, a good opponent can 3-bet you every time with any two cards and profit. If it is very low, you may be calling or 4-betting with hands that do not deserve it. Compare it with your [4-bet%](/blog/4-bet-percentage-poker-stat-explained) to see how you use the non-folds.

## One caution

This is a rare spot. You open maybe fifteen times per hundred hands and get 3-bet only in a small part of them, so a number built on 20 or 30 opportunities can swing a lot. See [how many hands you need before your stats are reliable](/blog/sample-size-reliable-poker-stats) before you decide that an opponent folds too much.
