## What RFI measures

**Raise first in** (RFI), also called the open-raise percentage, is how often you raise when everyone before you has folded. It measures how wide you open your range from each seat.

```
RFI% = times you raised first in / times you had the chance to raise first in
```

The chance exists only when no one has entered the pot before you (no limps and no raises). Hands where someone limped or raised first do not count.

## RFI is not the same as PFR

Your [PFR](/blog/understanding-vpip-pfr-3bet-hud-stats) counts every preflop raise, including 3-bets and 4-bets. RFI counts only the first raise in an unopened pot. Two players can have the same PFR with very different RFI, so RFI is a cleaner view of how wide you open.

## Why it must be read by position

A single RFI number mixes seats that should be played very differently. As a very rough guide for 100 big blind 6-max cash games, many strong players open around 15% from under the gun, a bit wider in the hijack, around a quarter to a third from the cutoff, and well above 40% from the button. The small blind and big blind are special cases. Treat these as a direction, because the right range depends on the game, the stack sizes and the players behind you.

## A worked example

On the button you faced an unopened pot 200 times and raised 94 of them. Your button RFI is 94 / 200 = 47%.

## Reading it on opponents

- **A very tight early-position RFI.** When a tight player opens from early position, their range is strong. Respect it and fold your weaker hands.
- **A very wide late-position RFI.** Players who open almost everything from the button can be 3-bet more often, especially if their [fold to 3-bet](/blog/fold-to-3bet-poker-stat-explained) is high.
- **A player whose RFI barely changes by position.** They are not adjusting, so their early-position range is too loose and their late-position range is too tight.

## Using it on yourself

If your early-position RFI is far above the number you intend to open, you are playing too many hands out of position and losing money before the flop. If your button RFI is low, you are leaving profit on the table in the best seat. Compare your RFI by position with your [steal percentage](/blog/steal-and-fold-to-steal-poker-stats) and with your [VPIP](/blog/understanding-vpip-pfr-3bet-hud-stats) to see where the gaps are.

## One caution

RFI needs a few thousand hands in total, and a few hundred from each seat, before it is stable. [How many hands you need before your stats are reliable](/blog/sample-size-reliable-poker-stats) explains how to judge that.
