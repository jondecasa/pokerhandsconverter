## Two different tools

Both programs import your hands, compute stats and run a HUD. They are built for different players: DriveHUD is aimed at simplicity and price, PokerTracker 4 at depth and customisation. Pricing and features below are taken from each company's own site at the time of writing, so always check the current pages.

## At a glance

| | DriveHUD | PokerTracker 4 |
|---|---|---|
| Positioning | Easier to start, lower price | More reports and customisation |
| Platforms | Windows (Mac only through a Windows emulator, per its FAQ) | Windows and macOS |
| Free trial | 14 days | 14 days |
| Price | Tiered by stake, e.g. a first-year price that then drops (see its [pricing page](https://drivehud.com/pricing/)) | Paid licence, with editions that vary, see its [purchase page](https://www.pokertracker.com/products/PT4/purchase.php) |
| Extras it advertises | Built-in solver and trainer, automated leak analysis | HUD editor, custom stats and reports, LeakTracker, ICM and equity tools |

A price that is tiered by stake means the cheaper plan covers lower buy-ins only. If you play higher, compare the plan that matches your stakes, not the headline figure.

## Who each suits

- **DriveHUD** suits players who want a tracker that is quick to set up and cheaper to run, especially at lower stakes, and who like built-in analysis tools.
- **PokerTracker 4** suits players who want to build their own filters and reports, or who work on a Mac. It also has a long-standing user community and plenty of guides. [PokerTracker 4 vs Hold'em Manager 3](/blog/pokertracker-4-vs-holdem-manager-3-coinpoker) and [Hand2Note vs PokerTracker 4](/blog/hand2note-vs-pokertracker-4) compare it with the other common options.

## Check CoinPoker support before you buy

This is the part that matters if you play on CoinPoker. CoinPoker doesn't write real-time hand histories, and its Third-Party Tools & Software policy has changed what trackers can do. PokerTracker's release notes say PokerTracker 4.18.25, from August 2026, [removed CoinPoker auto-import and HUD support](https://www.pokertracker.com/forums/viewtopic.php?f=58&t=103903&p=381149) to comply with that policy, while manual import of hands still works.

DriveHUD's FAQ lists CoinPoker among the sites it supports. Given the policy, **confirm the current status with DriveHUD** before paying, and check whether what you want is a live HUD or just importing hands afterwards.

If you only need to import downloaded hands, both tools can read a file once it's in a format they accept. Here is how it works in [PokerTracker 4](/blog/import-coinpoker-hands-pokertracker-4) and in [Hold'em Manager 3](/blog/import-coinpoker-hands-holdem-manager-3), and [PokerHandsConverter](/) prepares the file for either.

## The practical way to choose

Use the free trial of each on a small set of your own hands and see which one you open without thinking. Whichever you choose, the numbers are only as useful as the sample behind them.
