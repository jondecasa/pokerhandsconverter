If **PokerStars.es is not working** for you since the latest update, you are not alone. PokerStars España has migrated its website and its app, and players are reporting errors on both: pages and tabs that do not load, login trouble, and above all **balances that do not show up**. This page separates what is confirmed from what players are reporting, and gives you a checklist for the meantime.

We are not affiliated with PokerStars. This article is based on public statements and player reports, and we will update it as more is confirmed.

## What PokerStars has said

The official PokerStars España account on X acknowledged that it had carried out a **major migration**, that "a few screws are still to be tightened", and that it knows some users are running into problems and is working to fix them. You can read the post on [PokerStars España's X account](https://x.com/PokerStarsSpain/status/2118078214979428630).

At the time of writing we have not found an official post that explains what exactly changed or confirms that every problem is closed. Treat anything beyond that statement as unconfirmed.

## What players are reporting

These are reports from users, not confirmed by PokerStars:

- **Balance not visible** in the app after the update, including the balance of daily spin rewards.
- **Funds that seem blocked** for a couple of days, with no answer inside the app.
- **Errors loading the web or the app**, empty tabs and pages that fail to open.
- **Login problems** after the new version was installed.

A similar thing happened after the July 2024 maintenance. In that case the balance of some players showed as zero for a while and PokerStars later said the pending problems had been resolved, as [TechRadar reported](https://global.techradar.com/es-es/computing/websites-apps/pokerstars-esta-dando-problemas-el-saldo-de-la-gente-ha-desaparecido-esto-es-lo-que-se-sabe). It is not proof of what is happening now, but it shows that a balance that does not display is often a display issue and not a lost balance.

## A checklist while it is being fixed

1. **Do not repeat deposits or withdrawals.** If something looks stuck, doing it again can leave you with two pending operations. Wait and check your account history first.
2. **Update the app only from the official store** and fully close and reopen it. Reboot your device if the error persists.
3. **Try the other way in.** If the app fails, test the website in a browser, and the other way round. In earlier large updates, some players could log in from the web when the app failed.
4. **Clear the cache or reinstall.** Remove the app, restart and install the latest version from the official page.
5. **Check the official troubleshooting guide.** PokerStars publishes a [guide to error codes](https://www.pokerstars.es/poker/sala/errores-de-funcionamiento) (for example 2/67, 2/83 or 2/87), which usually point to a firewall, security software or the connection. Turn off a VPN or proxy while you test.
6. **Take screenshots with the date and time** of the error, your balance and any pending operation, then contact support with them. Written evidence speeds things up.
7. **Follow the official channels** ([PokerStars España on X](https://x.com/pokerstarsspain)) for updates before you assume anything about your funds.

## If you use a tracker: check your hand histories

After a big client change it is worth a quick check that your hands are still being saved where your tracker expects them. Play a few hands, then see whether they appear in PokerTracker, Hold'em Manager or Hand2Note. If you notice gaps, note the dates: hands played during an incident can be incomplete, and a clean record is easier to fix at the time than weeks later. For a routine to review sessions once things settle, see [how to review your hands without a HUD](/blog/review-poker-hands-without-a-hud).
