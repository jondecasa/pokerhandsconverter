## 4-bet meaning

A **4-bet** is the third raise in a betting round, and the fourth "bet" if you count the blinds as the first. In practice it is a re-raise of a 3-bet, almost always made before the flop.

The preflop bet ladder is:

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| 1-bet | The big blind |
| 2-bet | The first raise (the open raise) |
| 3-bet | A re-raise of the open |
| 4-bet | A re-raise of the 3-bet |
| 5-bet | A re-raise of the 4-bet, often all-in |

Counting the blind as the first bet is why a plain open raise is a "2-bet" and a re-raise is a "3-bet".

## An example with sizes

Blinds are 0.5 and 1. The player under the gun opens to 3 big blinds. The button 3-bets to 9. The original raiser 4-bets to 21. That last raise is the 4-bet. At this point the player who 3-bet can fold, call, or 5-bet all-in.

## Why players 4-bet

- **For value.** With hands like aces, kings and ace-king, you want more money in the pot against someone who is already showing strength.
- **As a bluff.** Hands that block your opponent's strongest holdings, such as a suited ace, are good 4-bet bluffs, because you hold one of the cards that makes a strong hand for them. The bluff works when the opponent folds often enough. See [fold to 4-bet](/blog/fold-to-4bet-poker-stat-explained) for the break-even math.
- **To stop being exploited.** If you never 4-bet, anyone can 3-bet you without fear.

## 4-bet percentage

The stat shows how often a player 4-bets when they have the chance. It is a narrow range for most players, because 4-bets come only after a 3-bet. [4-bet percentage](/blog/4-bet-percentage-poker-stat-explained) explains the number, and [fold to 3-bet](/blog/fold-to-3bet-poker-stat-explained) shows the other side of the same fight.

## How to respond to a 4-bet

- **When someone 4-bets you, think about their range.** A tight player's 4-bet is mostly premium hands. A player with a high 4-bet% has bluffs in the range.
- **Position and stack depth matter.** Deep stacks and a position advantage let you continue with more hands.
- **Do not auto-fold everything.** If you fold to every 4-bet, good players will 3-bet you and 4-bet bluff you for free.

## One caution

4-bets are rare, so any 4-bet stat needs a large sample. [How many hands you need before your stats are reliable](/blog/sample-size-reliable-poker-stats) shows why a few hundred hands tell you very little.
